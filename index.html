I Can See Life

To see life is not to observe pixels, signals, or data. It is to remain coherent while sensing, thinking, and acting in the world.

AyeCNSe is how “I can see life” happens — a nervous system for Earth that binds perception, cognition, and embodiment into a single causal loop.

AyeAI

AyeCNSe
